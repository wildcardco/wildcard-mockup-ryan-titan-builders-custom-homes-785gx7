Who We Are

A Handshake and a Blueprint

Titan Builders Custom Homes is a small, high-trust builder based in Wheatfield, Indiana. We are not a volume production company and we do not run a call center. When you call, you talk to the builder.

We take on a limited number of homes so each one gets owner-level attention. That focus is why every client who has reviewed us has given five stars.

Our promise is simple: permanence and craftsmanship. A house built right, on your land, that stands for generations.
